About me

PhD Scholar in Cognitive Science at IIT Hyderabad, exploring the neuroscience of motor learning and memory.

Who I am

I am a Ph.D. Scholar in the Department of Liberal Arts at IIT Hyderabad, working in the area of Cognitive Science. My research sits at the intersection of cognitive psychology and neuroscience, with a focus on understanding how the brain acquires, consolidates, and transfers motor skills.

I am particularly interested in the role of working memory in motor learning — how cognitive resources shape the way we form, retain, and generalize movement memories across limbs and contexts.
